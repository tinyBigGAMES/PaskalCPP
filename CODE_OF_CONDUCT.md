# PaskalCPP Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PaskalCPP aims to be a welcoming, constructive community for developers of all experience levels. We want people to learn, exchange ideas, report problems, and improve the project without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants with respect and patience.
- Discuss technical decisions on their merits, even when you strongly disagree.
- Offer useful feedback and accept good-faith criticism.
- Help newcomers find relevant documentation and discussions when possible.
- Respect others' privacy and intellectual property.
- Keep issues, pull requests, and project forums focused on productive discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
- Discriminatory insults or hateful conduct directed at individuals or groups.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

Strong technical opinions and respectful disagreement are welcome. Criticism of code or design is not, by itself, misconduct.

## Scope

This code applies in official PaskalCPP community spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and official community channels. It also applies when someone is formally representing the project in a project-related setting.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, report it privately to the maintainers by email at support [at] paskalcpp [dot] org. Where available, use GitHub's moderation and reporting features.

Include relevant context and links when possible. Do not post sensitive personal information or harassment reports publicly merely to request help.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably practical. Information may be shared when necessary to investigate a report, protect participants, or comply with legal obligations; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take proportionate action based on the circumstances, including:

1. A private request to change behavior or a warning.
2. Removal or moderation of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Permanent removal from official community spaces for serious or repeated misconduct.

Decisions will consider context, severity, prior behavior, and community safety. Maintainers should avoid conflicts of interest where practical.

## Questions

For questions about this code, email support [at] paskalcpp [dot] org.
