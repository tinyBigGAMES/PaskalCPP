## Original project

PaskalCPP was originally created by Jarrod Davis and is developed and maintained by tinyBigGAMES™ LLC, together with community contributors.

PaskalCPP is a Pascal/Oberon-inspired systems programming language and development system with C++ interoperability. The project includes a compiler, language server, editor, and related development tools.
